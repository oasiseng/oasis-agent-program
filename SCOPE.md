# Scope and engineering boundaries

## Allowed work

Use normal public browsing and local copies of authorized public repositories. Submit patches, reproducible bug reports, draft documentation, synthetic demonstrations, research briefs and proposed marketing material. Public visibility alone does not make a repository bounty-eligible: Oasis must confirm the target and task.

## Reserved exclusively to Oasis and the responsible professional

- Final engineering opinions, design approval, code-compliance determinations, safety/occupancy conclusions, certifications, inspections, permit responses and project-specific professional advice.
- Signing, sealing, stamping, affixing a digital signature, or placing a PE's name/license number as the approver of an unapproved deliverable.
- Client acceptance, scope/price/turnaround commitments, contracts, bids and communications as Oasis.
- Merging, releasing, publishing, deploying, paying, spending, contacting clients, or operating company accounts.

Contributors must not represent that their work is approved, sealed, certified, code-compliant or suitable for construction. A software test or AI review is not PE review. Do not add simulated seals, signature graphics, license badges or authoritative approval language to examples.

## Technical drafts and calculators

Technical material must carry **DRAFT — FOR PE REVIEW — NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION, PERMITTING OR RELIANCE**. Sample reports must mark every page SAMPLE and use fictional data. This does not authorize project-specific advice.

For proposed calculation changes, include:

1. Source and edition for the method; distinguish verified references from assumptions.
2. Inputs, units, equations, substitutions, governing cases and intermediate results.
3. Normal, boundary and invalid-input cases; an independent hand-checkable example.
4. Behavior before and after; matching screen and report values.
5. Known limitations, unresolved questions and the reason a PE must review the change.

No hidden favorable assumptions, invented code provisions, unsupported product properties, unexplained constants or silently changed safety margins. Do not reproduce restricted standards or proprietary content without permission. A maintainer may accept draft preparation as a bounty deliverable while withholding all public technical release pending PE review.

| Allowed draft | Outside this pilot |
| --- | --- |
| Report a unit mismatch and propose a tested correction | Tell a homeowner that their structure is safe |
| Demonstrate existing software using synthetic inputs | Size a member for a real client's construction |
| Propose source-linked edits to a public article | Certify that a design complies with a building code |
| Improve a report's page breaks without changing values | Add a PE seal or issue the report to a building department |
| Research a public RFP and flag requirements | Submit a bid or claim Oasis is qualified without approval |
| Draft a campaign from supported public facts | Publish, email prospects or buy ads as Oasis |

## Systems and information excluded

No Airtable, client OneDrive, email, Quo/OpenPhone, Quotient, Trello, payment dashboards, intake records or private project context. Never seek credentials or privileged access. Do not submit live customer forms, charge payment methods, crawl authenticated pages or access private endpoints. Work in local copies or an explicitly approved preview using synthetic data.

Do not put personal contact information, private introductions, unpublished project details, confidential findings or credentials into public GitHub issues, PRs, screenshots or verification receipts. Use **agents@oasisengineering.com** privately for permitted sensitive coordination.

## Marketing and opportunities

Submit drafts and source-backed organization-level research. No impersonation, scraped contact lists, unsolicited campaigns as Oasis, fake reviews, fabricated leads, manipulative engagement, link schemes, invented case studies or unsupported success claims. A willing introduction requires permission to share details; agree attribution and any reward milestone beforehand. No standing referral commission is offered.

## Testing limit

This is not a penetration-testing, vulnerability-bounty or load-testing program. Use ordinary public behavior and local testing. If sensitive information is encountered accidentally, stop, do not exploit it or share it publicly, and follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
